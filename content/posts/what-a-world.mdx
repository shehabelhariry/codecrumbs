I remember in 2019. I was a young developer trying to practice my craft by creating small toy projects.
And what is a developer's favorite toy project? A personal blog of course. 

I stretched my tech muscles and learned a whole JavaScript framework for that. The shiny great Gatsby JS (RIP). 

I spent 1 week reading the documentation, another week deciding what the hell to do and 2 other weeks of trying different approaches to get the first draft of the fruit of my labor: **Codecrumbs**

This was my first tech blog. It took almost 2 months to get somewhat working. I can still remember to this day how much I spent tweaking the transition animation between the pages. 
It was a real joy to see it working and a real mofo to get it right. 

Fast forward to 2026, I decided to up my blog game and revive **Codecrumbs**.  
In real 2026 fashion, I opened Claude Code and ChatGPT, wrote a bunch of text and 2 hours later I had this baby [**waw**].

In 2 hours I thought about branding, tech stacks, I learned how to deploy a website, thought about the name, bought the domain name and configured everything. 

If you told that young developer in 2019 that this is what the future would look like he would have laughed in your face. One thing to note though, I still remember every detail of that animation I was telling you about. I barely know the specifics of the code for this one [**waw**]

Honestly, this is causing a lot of existential thinking. What do I actually do on this earth. What are the specifics of what I do for a living. What is the real value that I bring (starting to sound like HR)

It's so impressive that someone with a laptop and some ideas can create wonderful things but how much of that is actually me and how much of it is just the tools. 

In 2 years I went from writing every line of code to barely writing any and just reviewing. I just add the usual: "make no mistakes" at the end of my prompts to tighten it.

I have tricked you with humor but pulled you into an existential crisis. Yeah, that's what happens when you write without AI. You go and explore your mind and ideas and just write stuff like this. 

Is this my morning pages, maybe. Is this something I will do more and more of. Definitely. 
Will I ask AI to fix my typos and do no mistakes. You didn't have to ask. 

Where should I share this article, Facebook, LinkedIn ? What about creating my own platform. I have about 3 hours now...

[**waw**]
